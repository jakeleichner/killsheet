About KillSheet

KillSheet is a free companion app for Kill Team, on your phone or computer.

  • Build a roster for any of the 48 teams, with the team's limits checked.
  • Look up the core rules, ploys, equipment and weapon rules, with diagrams for cover, visibility and control range.
  • Track wounds, orders, command points and victory points during a game.
  • Shoot and fight from your datacards, with attack and defense dice.
  • Play missions from Games Workshop's free mission packs, with their maps.
  • Play online with a friend on another device.
  • Sign in, if you like, to back up your rosters, games and settings and use them on any device.

It works offline once it has loaded, and you can add it to your Home Screen.
