Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

KillSheet is a free, unofficial companion app for Kill Team, made by one player as a hobby. It has no ads and doesn't sell or share your information for marketing. This page says what it stores and why.

Without an account

Your rosters, games and settings are saved in your browser, on your device. They don't leave it unless you choose to: by backing up to a file, sharing a roster code, or playing online. No account is needed to use KillSheet.

If you make an account

  • Your email address, to sign you in. If you use Google, Google tells us your email address and a Google account ID, and nothing else.
  • Your saved rosters, game history and settings, so they're backed up and show up on your other devices. This includes any names you give your operatives. A game in progress isn't uploaded.
  • A sign-in cookie, which keeps you signed in. It isn't used for tracking. Signing in with Google also uses a second cookie for up to 10 minutes while you do it.

There are no passwords to leak: KillSheet doesn't have any. When you sign in with email, we send a code that works for 15 minutes.

Deleting your account

In KillSheet, go to Settings, Account, then Delete my account. That deletes your email address and cloud backup straight away, including the older backup copy we keep for recovery. What's saved on your own device stays until you clear it.

If you send feedback

Send feedback stores your message, the reply address if you give one, the KillSheet version, your device type (such as "iPhone, Safari"), your screen size and the tab you were on. If you report a crash, it also stores the error.

If KillSheet crashes, it sends a crash report on its own: the error, the KillSheet version, your device type, screen size and the tab you were on. Nothing that identifies you. You can turn this off in Settings, under Privacy. These reports and feedback are only used to fix problems and improve the app, and are deleted once they've been dealt with.

Keeping things fair

To stop abuse, KillSheet limits how many messages and sign-in codes can be sent. It does that with a scrambled code made from your IP address and the date, which changes every day. It doesn't store your IP address itself.

Other services KillSheet uses

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores account data and feedback.
  • Resend sends the sign-in emails, so it receives your email address.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with it. Google Fonts also supplies the app's fonts.
  • cdnjs and unpkg supply some of the code the app loads.
  • PeerJS and ExpressTURN help two devices connect when you play online. Your game goes between the two devices and isn't stored.

Like any website, these services can see your IP address when your device connects to them.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and older. Younger players can use KillSheet without an account, which keeps everything on their device. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date. Questions, or a request about your information: killsheet-support@googlegroups.com.
